Journey to Maizy

The story is about a farmer who is following the maizes to get to his daughter who was stolen along with his corn. The corns are  the trail left behind by Maizy.

A group of bandits came to steal corn from the farmer's land but in doing so took away his daughter maizy as well.

The ball is his hat which will be revealed later.

After every maze, you, the player inch closer to reaching Maizy.

Users are shown all the mazes they collected. Every new stage is a new terrain that the user goes through. Users don't know that they are actually doing this, they just think it is a different way of making things interesting.

We also need a dark forest illuminated by fireflies. It can be neon effect where the squares closest to the player is lit up.

We also need a windy place where there are gusts of wind that blow occasionally that cause the user a little harder to move.

And also terrains of snow where the roads are slippery.

And also terrains of desert where everything is bright and its hard to walk.

You make a bargain with the main bandit at the end to trade all the maize you have collected so far in exchange for Maizy.

After the player makes the bargain, the Bandit takes the Maize and tell the farmer, he was too late. If only you were faster, you could have saved her. Which begins the speedrun. Where the users can again go through the maizes and if they beat their previous time in every maize, they will save Maizy.

At the end, the father thanks the player. "Thank you for helping me reach my daughter".
